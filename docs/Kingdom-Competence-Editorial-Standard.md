# Full Circle — Kingdom competence editorial standard

Scripture is the primary curriculum. Treat the biblical text as authoritative within the Christian formation program. Do not present biblical accounts as mythology. Distinguish the text's explicit claims, historical evidence, instructor interpretation, supplementary authors and practical applications. Never invent evidence or certainty.

## Instructional purpose
Develop ethical leadership, spiritual discernment, stewardship, institutional competence, disciplined service, accountability, character, voluntary discipleship and responsibility under God's authority. Historical military accounts are studied in their historical and spiritual context; practical applications emphasize nonviolent organizational leadership and spiritual formation, not coercion or territorial domination.

## Categories
- Context: preceding events, relevant institutions, causes, decisions and consequences.
- Historical: identifiable evidence, geography, political economy, original-language terms with verifiable sources.
- Spiritual: allegiance, sonship, obedience, Kingdom authority and responsibility.
- Present-day: concrete applications to organizational governance, resource stewardship, leadership and ethical service.
- Did You Know: distinct, consequential facts, not generic restatements.
- Threads: classify reviewed connections individually; do not infer fulfilment from similarity. Unreviewed links remain Related passage. Labels and colors must both be visible.
- Chapter Summary: decisions, outcomes, institutional failures, competencies, questions and verse anchors.
- Exploration: answer from textual evidence when possible; leave genuinely unresolved questions open.

## Sources
Scripture first. Use supplied works of Rick Joyner, Myles Munroe and Jonathan Cahn only after inspecting the actual relevant material. Attribute interpretations, avoid extensive reproduction, and do not equate an author's interpretation with Scripture. Rick Joyner's The Final Quest emphasizes obedience to the Spirit and love as a spiritual weapon; distinguish this visionary account from biblical history. Do not attribute specific claims to other books without reviewing them.

## Acceptance
A reader should acquire an identifiable competence or meaningful factual insight. Avoid repetitive filler, unsupported historical claims, and generic devotional prose. No fabricated chapter summaries. Review entries against their actual verse references before publication.
